Method

Sentinel-2 L1C (harmonised) · Cloud Score+ masking · January–May median composites · spectral indices (NDVI, NDBI, MNDWI, BSI) · Random Forest trained on areas where ESA WorldCover and Dynamic World agree · temporal consistency filter · validation on photo-interpreted points.
